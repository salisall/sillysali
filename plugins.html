#PLUGINS

Useful plugins and connectors that let AI work inside Drive, Notion, Canva, your inbox and more.

#skills#tools#plugins#showcase
Updated Oct 2026 · AI moves fast, things may have changed

Plugins and connectors plug AI into the apps you already use, so it can read your files and do real work. Turn them on in your AI app's settings.

🗂01

Google Drive connector

Let your AI read your Docs, Sheets and PDFs directly instead of copy-pasting.
