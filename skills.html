#SKILLS

8 skills that make every AI tool better. No coding needed for most of them, and each one has something to try today.

#skills#tools#plugins#showcase
Updated Oct 2026 · AI moves fast, things may have changed
✍️01

Clear prompting

Tell the AI who it is, what's going on, and exactly what you want back. Most "bad answers" are really unclear questions.

TRY THIS ✦Use 4 parts: Role + Context + Result + Format. e.g. "You're a social media editor. I sell handmade candles. Write 3 IG captions under 30 words, casual tone."
📎02

Giving good context

Attach files, paste examples, share what you've tried. AI works best when it can see what you see.

TRY THIS ✦Paste one example you love and say "write it like this". Examples beat long explanations.
🔍03

Checking the answer

AI can sound confident and still be wrong. Check facts, numbers and links before you use them, especially for anything important.

TRY THIS ✦Ask: "Which parts of this are you least sure about?" and "Give me sources I can check."
🧩04

Building reusable Skills

If you type the same instructions again and again, save them once: Claude Skills, Projects, custom GPTs or Gems remember how you like things done.

TRY THIS ✦Pick one task you do weekly (e.g. meeting notes → to-do list) and turn your best prompt into a Skill.
🪜05

Breaking work into steps

Big asks give messy results. Split the job (outline → draft → edit) and check each step. It's also how AI agents work.

TRY THIS ✦Start with: "Before writing, give me a 5-point outline and wait for my OK."
💻06

Vibe coding basics

You can build small websites and tools by describing them. Knowing a little about files, HTML and how to test helps a lot.

TRY THIS ✦Ask for "one single HTML file" for your first project. It's the easiest thing to open and share.
📊07

Reading data with AI

Upload a spreadsheet and ask questions in plain words. Knowing what a fair comparison looks like keeps you from wrong conclusions.

TRY THIS ✦Ask: "Summarise this sheet in 3 insights, then show the chart that proves each one."
🔒08

Privacy sense

Don't paste passwords, ID numbers, client secrets or other people's private info. Check your tool's data settings once.

TRY THIS ✦Swap real names and numbers for placeholders like [CLIENT] or [AMOUNT] before pasting.
